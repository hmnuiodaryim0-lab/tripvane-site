A little more context

Designed for the days between departure and home.

05

Know when a forecast deserves attention.

TripVane evaluates upcoming itinerary weather for heavy rain, snow, thunderstorms, strong wind, heat and cold. It is a travel planning aid and should not be used as the sole source for safety-critical decisions.

06

A daily check-in before departure.

Daily Trip Brief can provide a local notification summarizing recent weather information. Delivery timing and background updates depend on iOS scheduling.

07

Plan beyond your current region.

MapKit search and a bundled offline global city index help keep city search useful across regions and network conditions. Weather still needs a network connection.

08

Your past trips stay with you.

Completed trips move into Trip History with saved forecasts, forecast changes and weather-risk history—not actual observed historical weather.

Privacy by design

Your itinerary stays on your device.

No account is required. Trip data is stored locally on your device. TripVane does not operate a backend that receives your itinerary or location data. Location access is optional and used only for trip-progress features when enabled.
