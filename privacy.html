Privacy, by design

TripVane Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

TripVane is designed as a local-first travel weather application. This policy explains how information is handled when you use TripVane.

2. Accounts

No account or registration is required.

3. Trip Data

Trips, destinations, dates, saved forecasts, forecast changes and risk records are stored locally on your device.

4. Developer Data Collection

TripVane does not send user trip records to a developer-operated server. TripVane has no developer analytics backend, advertising SDK or tracking SDK.

5. Location

Location permission is optional. When enabled, location may be used to help determine trip progress. TripVane does not maintain a developer-operated server that receives location history. Location and weather services provided by Apple may process requests according to Apple's privacy policies.

6. Apple Services

TripVane uses Apple frameworks and services including WeatherKit, MapKit, Core Location, BackgroundTasks, UserNotifications and WidgetKit. Requests to Apple services may be processed according to Apple's privacy policies.

7. Weather Data

Weather forecasts may be requested based on destination coordinates. Weather information can change and is not guaranteed.

8. Notifications

TripVane uses local notifications. TripVane does not operate a remote push notification service.

9. Background Processing

iOS may allow background refresh. Timing is controlled by the operating system and is not guaranteed.

10. Widgets

TripVane and its Widget Extension share a limited cached widget snapshot using an Apple App Group container on the device.

11. GeoNames

TripVane includes a bundled city database containing data derived in part from GeoNames. GeoNames is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

12. Third-party Advertising and Analytics

TripVane does not use third-party advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

13. Children

TripVane is not specifically directed at children.

14. Data Deletion

Because TripVane does not require an account or developer server, users may remove trips in the app or remove locally stored app data by deleting the app.

15. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if TripVane's privacy practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

16. Contact
